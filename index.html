01 — IN THE BOX

Eleven things come out of the box.

Check them off before you start. The two heads that touch the floor are the multi-surface brush and the spin mop head. The three small tools click straight onto the body for handheld cleaning.

02 — ASSEMBLE

Three clicks and a charge.

Every joint on this vacuum locks with an audible click and releases with a grey button. Press play, or tap a step to jump to it.

Wall mount: stick the template about 1 m above the floor at the height marked on it, drill two 8 mm holes, add the plugs and screws. Hang the vacuum with the rod and brush attached, never the body alone.

04 — USE IT

Hold the trigger. Lock it. Pick a mode.

This is a live copy of the display on the back of the motor. Hold the trigger to run, tap the lock to keep running without holding, and cycle the mode button under the screen. Battery drains at demo speed: one second here is one minute of cleaning.

Stopped
≈ 90 min left in Eco

Eco: quietest and longest. Daily hard floors and light dust.

  • ▸Hold to run, release to stop. That is the whole on/off logic. Tap the lock while running and the vacuum keeps going with your finger off the trigger; tap it again to go back.
  • ▸Mode button sits under the screen. Each tap cycles Eco → Standard → Turbo. The display shows the level and the battery percentage.
  • ▸Slow overlapping passes beat fast ones. Let the roller and the front LEDs do the work. On carpet, flip the brush switch on.
  • ▸It stops itself for a reason. A stuck roller or a hot motor shuts the vacuum off. Clear the jam or wait for it to cool, then continue. After long Turbo runs, let it cool 30 minutes before charging.
05 — MOP

Vacuum and mop in one pass, on hard floors only.

The spin mop head carries a 200 ml tank and two rotating pads at 116 rpm. It suits linoleum, laminate, parquet and tile. Keep it off carpet and rugs.

Outflow
Never mop carpet, rugs or play mats. Do not step on the mop head. Empty the tank and set the outflow to Off before storing it.
06 — CLEAN & CARE

Suction lives or dies on five minutes of care.

Everything washable is rinsed with plain water and air-dried for 24 hours before it goes back in. Nothing gets detergent, and the body, the brush motors and the battery never get wet.

08 — SAFETY

The manual's warnings, in one place.

Everything the safety pages say, grouped by situation. The short version: dry floors only, nothing hot or sharp, the original charger, and no water near anything with a motor.

Before you start
  • Dry indoor floors only. Never outdoors, in a bathroom or near a pool.
  • Look over the battery and the charger before use. If either is damaged, do not use it.
  • Brushes, dust cup and filter must be fitted properly before you switch on.
  • Clear the floor of large paper, plastic sheeting and anything sharp or bigger than the openings.
  • Home use only, with Dreame accessories only.
Never vacuum
  • Liquids of any kind: water, drinks, petrol, bleach, ammonia, drain cleaner.
  • Anything hot or smoking: fireplace ash, coal, cigarette butts, matches.
  • Sharp or hard objects: glass, nails, screws, coins.
  • Drywall dust and fine building debris.
Hands, hair and kids
  • Keep hair, loose clothing and fingers away from the openings and the spinning roller.
  • Never point the rod or a tool at eyes, ears or mouth.
  • Not a toy. Children from 8 and anyone with reduced abilities only under supervision; a child never cleans or maintains it alone.
  • Take extra care on stairs.
  • Do not lean it against a chair or table where it can fall over.
Battery and charging
  • Only the original Dreame charger and battery pack. Others can make the lithium battery catch fire.
  • Charge, use and store between 0 and 40 °C.
  • After long Turbo use, let it cool 30 minutes before charging.
  • It will not run while plugged in. Unplug before use, and unplug before any cleaning or maintenance.
  • Not using it for a while? Charge fully, unplug, store cool and dry, and top up every 3 months.
Water and electricity
  • The body, both brush heads, the battery and the metal contact pins conduct electricity: never put them in water, and never touch them or the charger with wet hands.
  • Only the filters, cyclone, pre-filter, brush rollers and mop pads go under the tap, and they dry for 24 hours before going back.
  • Clean water only in the mop tank. No detergent, no chemicals, and no fragrance on the filter: that is a fire hazard.
  • Do not step on the mop head, and use only Dreame pads.
If something goes wrong
  • A jammed roller or a hot motor shuts the vacuum off by itself. Remove the object or wait for it to cool, then carry on.
  • Dropped or damaged? Stop using it and contact Dreame service. Never open the body yourself.
  • Battery leaking or fuming: leave the room until it cools and the fumes clear, ventilate, avoid skin and eye contact, do not breathe the vapour.
  • End of life: remove the battery pack and hand both it and the vacuum to an electrical-waste collection point, never household rubbish.
